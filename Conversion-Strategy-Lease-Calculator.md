# Finosutra — How to Convert Free Users into Paying Customers
### Strategy for the IND AS 116 / IFRS 16 Lease Calculator
*Prepared for Krishna · 12 June 2026 · For discussion, not yet built*

---

## 1. The real problem (reframe)

The copy-block idea is solving the wrong problem.

The problem is **not** "users copy our output."
The problem is **"users get 100% of the value for free, so they have zero reason to pay."**

Blocking text selection only stops casual mouse-highlighting. Anyone can still right-click, screenshot, print, or open DevTools — so it doesn't protect anything. What it *does* do is frustrate the exact people most likely to pay (CAs and CFOs who genuinely want to drop a number into their working file). For a brand built on "premium, trusted, CFO-grade," fighting your own users is the wrong move.

**The cure:** stop hiding value behind a copy-block. Instead, build a clear free/paid line where the free tier *proves the math is right* and the paid tier *delivers the audit-ready deliverable they need for their job.*

---

## 2. What a CA actually pays for

They will not pay for "numbers on a screen." They will pay to **save hours** and **get audit-ready output**. The value sits in:

1. **Audit-ready export** — clean, formatted Excel/PDF they can drop straight into workpapers (client name, no watermark).
2. **Journal entries** — the actual Dr/Cr entries: initial recognition, monthly interest unwinding + depreciation, period-end.
3. **Disclosure notes** — ready IND AS 116 disclosure language (maturity analysis, etc.) for the financial statements.
4. **Multiple leases** — a saved portfolio they revisit every quarter and roll forward.
5. **Modifications / remeasurements** — rent revisions, extensions, indexation. This is the genuinely *hard* part of IND AS 116 and the strongest reason to pay.
6. **Continuity** — come back next period and the data is still there.

---

## 3. The free vs paid line (the key decision)

**Free (no login, or email only):**
Calculate one lease. See the on-screen summary — ROU asset, lease liability, finance cost, depreciation — plus a preview of the first 12 months of the schedule. Fully usable, copyable, no friction. This is the "wow, it's correct, this saves me time" moment. **Give it away generously** — it's your best marketing.

**Paid:**
Full schedule download (all periods), journal entries, disclosure notes, saved/multiple leases, remeasurement handling, clean branded export.

Why this beats copy-blocking: you're no longer fighting the user, you're *selling them the thing they already want*. The paywall sits exactly where willingness-to-pay is highest — the deliverable, not the preview.

---

## 4. Pricing model (launch fast, India-first)

A hybrid, because two different buyers exist:

- **Pay-per-report (micro):** ~₹199 for one audit-ready lease export. One-click UPI. Captures the one-off user who just needs a single lease done — lowest possible barrier to your first rupee.
- **Subscription (the real customer):** ~₹749/month or ~₹6,999/year for unlimited leases, saved portfolio, journal entries, disclosures, remeasurements. This is where recurring revenue lives — CAs reuse this every quarter, so **annual is the goal.**

Gateway: **Razorpay** (UPI, cards, and it supports subscriptions natively — right fit for India).

Anchor the price to *time saved*, not features: "Save 3+ hours per lease. ₹749/month." Against a CA's hourly rate, that's a no-brainer.

## 5. The conversion funnel (concrete)

1. **Land** → "Free IND AS 116 Lease Calculator. Audit-ready in 2 minutes." (Also your #1 SEO target — CAs google this exact phrase.)
2. **Calculate** → instant on-screen summary. Free, fully usable. This earns trust.
3. **Unlock** → to see the full schedule / journal entries / download: "Enter email to unlock" (lead capture) → then "Get audit-ready export ₹199 / Subscribe."
4. **Show what they're missing** → a blurred/teaser preview of the full branded Excel + the journal-entry table. *Seeing* the deliverable does the persuasion that copy-blocking never could.
5. **Checkout** → Razorpay → deliver the clean file instantly.
6. **Return** → "Save this lease to your portfolio (free account)" → builds the habit and the data lock-in.

## 6. Trust / CFO-grade elements

So they're comfortable paying:

- State the standard followed (IND AS 116 / Ind AS) and link a short methodology note.
- A worked example with a known answer they can verify against.
- Clean, minimal, professional UI — **no `cursor: not-allowed`**, which reads as "broken" or "hostile."
- One or two CA testimonials the moment you have them — social proof early.
- A clear privacy line: "We don't store your data unless you save it." CAs care deeply about client confidentiality — this is a selling point, not fine print.

## 9. The honest objection (so we're ready for it)

*"Why would a CA pay when free Excel templates exist?"*

Because templates break, don't handle remeasurements or disclosures, don't store a portfolio, and aren't audit-formatted. A tool that is *always correct*, handles the hard parts, and remembers their leases every quarter is worth a small subscription — **if** we price it well below what their time is worth. That's the whole bet. Keep the free tier honest and accurate so trust is never the blocker; put the paywall only on the deliverable.

---

### Bottom line
Drop the copy-block. Keep its *intent* (protect value) but move the wall to where it belongs: the audit-ready export and the journal entries. Users stay happy, and we actually get paid.
