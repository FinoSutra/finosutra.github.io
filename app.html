Getting Started

▼
1
Add a Company

Go to Companies in the sidebar → click + Add Company. Enter your client's legal name, GSTIN, FY start month (April for Indian entities), and reporting standard (IND AS). Each company acts as a reporting container for its leases.

2
Add Leases

Go to Leases → click + Add Lease. Fill the Inputs tab: lease name, lessor, asset class, commencement date, term, rent per period, IBR (discount rate), and link to the company you created. Then click Calculate.

3
Review the Results

After calculating, check the Schedules tab for the amortization table, Journal Entries for Day 1 and periodic JEs, and Disclosures for the Para 52 note. The Overview tab shows key figures at a glance.

4
Move through Workflow

Once you're satisfied, move the lease from Draft → Ready for Review → Reviewed → Approved using the status pill in the top-right of the lease workspace. The system checks conditions at each step.

5
Export

Click the Export Pack button (top-right of lease or company workspace) to open the Export Center. Choose Lease Pack, Company Pack, or Portfolio Pack depending on what you need.

Tip: Use Upload Template on the Leases page to bulk-add multiple leases at once via Excel — much faster for clients with 5+ leases.

Workflow Status — How It Works

▼

Every lease has a workflow status that tracks where it is in your review and approval process. Think of it like a document going from a CA's desk to a partner's sign-off.

Approved

Closed

If a lease is modified after approval, it moves to Modified and requires fresh review.

Draft Lease is being prepared or filled in. All fields are editable. Default for any new lease. No conditions. Preparer (CA / staff)
Ready for Review Lease inputs are complete and sent to the reviewer. ✅ No validation errors  ✅ Lease name & lessor filled  ✅ At least one calculation run  ✅ Company linked Preparer
Reviewed Reviewer has checked the lease and signed off. ✅ Reviewer name entered  ✅ Review date captured (Audit & Review tab → Sign-Off) Reviewer (senior CA)
Approved Partner / approver has approved. Lease is final. ✅ Lease must be Reviewed first  ✅ Approver name entered  ✅ Approval date captured Approver (partner / CFO)
Modified Lease terms changed after approval — needs fresh review. Can be set from any status. Signals that recalculation and re-review are needed. Anyone
Closed Lease has ended or been terminated. Can be set from Approved or Draft. Lease becomes inactive. Anyone
How to change status: Open any lease → look at the top-right pill (e.g. Draft). Click it to cycle, or use the action bar buttons like Mark for Review. If a condition isn't met, a red gate will appear telling you exactly what's missing.
Sign-Off fields are in the Audit & Review tab of each lease → scroll down to the Sign-Off section. Fill in Reviewer Name + Date before moving to Reviewed, and Approver Name + Date before moving to Approved. Click Save Sign-Off then Save.
?
Why is my status change blocked?

When you click a status button and see a red gate modal — read the list. Each item tells you exactly what's missing. Fix those items, then try again. If you only see yellow warnings (not red errors), an Override button will appear to force the change.

Company Workspace

▼

The Company Workspace aggregates all leases under one entity into a single view — useful for preparing consolidated disclosures for a client.

1
Open a Company

Go to Companies in the sidebar → click Open → on any company card.

2
Overview tab

See 15 KPIs across all leases: total liability, ROU asset, depreciation, workflow counts (how many Draft / Pending / Approved), alerts (missing docs, expiring leases, validation errors).

3
Leases tab

Filter by workflow status or lease status. Click any lease row to jump straight into that lease's workspace.

4
Disclosures tab

See the consolidated Para 52 maturity analysis across all company leases. Click Export Pack to export a full Excel disclosure pack.

Link leases to a company in the Inputs tab of each lease — look for the Company dropdown. Without this, leases won't appear in the Company Workspace.

Frequently Asked Questions

▼
Q
What IBR should I use?

IBR (Incremental Borrowing Rate) is the rate the lessee would pay to borrow funds to obtain an asset of similar value over a similar term. For Indian entities, typical range is 8–14% p.a. Use SBI MCLR + spread as a benchmark. Document your IBR basis in the Remarks field — this is an audit-sensitive judgment.

Q
What is the difference between Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Annual frequency?

This is how often the lessee makes rent payments. Select the frequency that matches your lease agreement. The engine calculates the periodic discount rate accordingly (annual IBR ÷ 12 for monthly, ÷ 4 for quarterly, etc.).

Q
What is Payment Timing — Beginning vs. End?

Beginning (Advance) means rent is paid at the start of each period (common for office leases in India — first cheque on day 1). End (Arrears) means rent is paid at the end. This affects the PV calculation — advance payments give a slightly lower liability.

Q
When should I use the Short-Term or Low-Value exemption?

Short-Term: Lease term (including renewal options) is 12 months or less. Low-Value: Underlying asset value when new is below ~₹5 lakh (approx. USD 5,000 per IFRS 16 guidance). When checked, the tool skips ROU/liability recognition and treats rent as straight-line P&L expense.

Q
My status change is blocked — what do I do?

Read the red gate modal carefully. Each blocker tells you exactly what's missing. Common fixes: (1) Run Calculate at least once before marking Ready for Review. (2) Fill Reviewer Name + Date in the Audit & Review tab before marking Reviewed. (3) A lease must be Reviewed before it can be Approved.

Q
How do I handle a lease modification (rent change, term extension)?

Open the lease → change the inputs (revised rent, revised term, revised IBR) → click Calculate. Set the workflow status to Modified and note the modification date and reason in the Remarks field. Then re-run the full review workflow: Modified → Ready for Review → Reviewed → Approved.

Q
What is ROU Asset and Lease Liability?

ROU (Right-of-Use) Asset: The asset recognised on Day 1 = Present value of future lease payments + Initial Direct Costs − Lease Incentives. It is depreciated on a straight-line basis over the lease term. Lease Liability: The present value of all future lease payments, unwound using the IBR. It reduces with each payment and increases with interest accrual.

Q
How do I export for audit?

Open the lease → click Export Pack button → select Working Paper Pack for a 5-sheet Excel workpaper (lease assessment, amortization, rollforward, JEs, IBR working). For a full Big-4 style 12-sheet pack, select Premium Report Pack.
