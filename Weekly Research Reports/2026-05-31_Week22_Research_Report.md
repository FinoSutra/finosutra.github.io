## 📋 REGULATORY UPDATES

**New Income Tax Act 2025 — Effective April 1, 2026**
- Replaces the Income Tax Act, 1961 entirely.
- Key change: "Assessment Year" is now "Tax Year." E.g., FY 2026-27 = Tax Year 2026-27.
- Sections condensed from 819 → 536. Forms from 390 → 190.
- MAT rate reduced from 15% to 14%, now treated as final tax.
- STT on futures more than doubled to 0.05%.
- **Action for Finosutra:** Update all calculator labels and help text that reference "Assessment Year (AY)" to reflect the new "Tax Year" terminology. Income Tax Calculator and In-Hand Salary Calculator are affected.

**IND AS Amendments — MCA Notification August 2025 (Effective FY 2026-27)**
- IND AS 10: Term "provision" replaced with "covenant" for consistency.
- IND AS 7 & 107: New disclosure requirements for supplier finance arrangements.
- IND AS 109 (your Security Deposit Calculator): Minor technical amendments — transitional provisions and paragraph reference corrections. No calculation logic changes required.
- **Action for Finosutra:** No immediate changes to existing calculator logic needed, but add a note in the Security Deposit Calculator that it reflects IND AS 109 as amended up to 2025.

**GST 2.0 — Effective September 22, 2025**
- Major rate restructuring: 12% slab abolished. Goods moved to either 5% or 18%.
- 57th GST Council Meeting expected June 2026 — possible further changes.
- **Action for Finosutra:** Verify your GST Calculator reflects the new slab structure (0%, 5%, 18%, 40%). Remove or flag any 12% references.
