Frequently Asked Questions
How do I calculate GST on a price?
To add GST: GST Amount = Base Price × GST Rate ÷ 100. Total = Base Price + GST Amount. To remove GST from an inclusive price: Base Price = Inclusive Price ÷ (1 + GST Rate÷100). Example: Remove 18% GST from ₹1,180 → ₹1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000 base price.
What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?
For intra-state transactions (within the same state), GST is split equally into CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST). For inter-state transactions (across states), IGST (Integrated GST) applies at the full rate. Example: 18% GST intra-state = 9% CGST + 9% SGST. Inter-state = 18% IGST.
What are the GST tax slabs in India?
GST has four main tax slabs: 5% (essential goods, transport), 12% (processed foods, business class air travel), 18% (most services, electronics, restaurants), and 28% (luxury goods, automobiles, aerated drinks). Some items like fresh food and healthcare are exempt (0%).
Can I claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on GST paid?
Yes, GST registered businesses can claim ITC on GST paid for purchases used in business. ITC can be used to offset your GST liability. ITC is available only if the supplier has filed their GSTR-1 and the invoice appears in your GSTR-2B.
What is the GST registration threshold?
Businesses with annual turnover above ₹40 lakh (goods) or ₹20 lakh (services) must register for GST. For special category states (North-East, Uttarakhand etc.), the threshold is ₹20 lakh (goods) and ₹10 lakh (services).
