Frequently Asked Questions
How is in-hand salary calculated from CTC?
In-hand salary = CTC minus employer PF (12% of Basic) minus gratuity provision minus TDS minus employee PF (12% of Basic) minus Professional Tax. Typical in-hand is 65–80% of CTC depending on your tax bracket and deductions.
What is the difference between CTC and gross salary?
CTC (Cost to Company) includes employer PF contribution and gratuity — amounts that don't come to you directly. Gross salary is your total salary before TDS and Professional Tax deductions. In-hand (net) salary is what you actually receive in your bank account.
Which tax regime gives higher in-hand salary?
The New Regime generally gives higher in-hand salary for people with fewer deductions. If your total deductions (80C + 80D + HRA etc.) are below ₹3.75 lakh, the New Regime usually results in lower TDS and higher take-home. Use this calculator to compare both regimes with your actual numbers.
Is Professional Tax deductible from income tax?
Yes. Professional Tax paid is fully deductible under Section 16(iii) of the Income Tax Act under the Old Regime. It is also allowed as a deduction under the New Regime. Maximum Professional Tax is ₹2,500 per year.
How is EPF (Employee PF) calculated?
Employee PF contribution is 12% of Basic salary (capped at ₹1,800/month if Basic exceeds ₹15,000). Employer also contributes 12% of Basic — 3.67% to EPF and 8.33% to EPS. Both contributions are part of your CTC but only the employee share reduces your in-hand salary.
What is the standard deduction for salaried employees in FY 2026-27?
Salaried employees get a standard deduction of ₹75,000 under the New Tax Regime for FY 2026-27. Under the Old Regime, the standard deduction is ₹50,000. This deduction is automatic — no bills or proofs are needed. It directly reduces your taxable salary income.
How much TDS is deducted from salary per month?
TDS on salary is calculated by estimating your full-year tax liability and dividing by 12. Your employer deducts TDS monthly based on your salary structure, declared investments (Form 12BB), and the regime you chose. Under the New Regime, if your annual taxable income is below ₹12 lakh, Section 87A rebate means zero tax and no TDS is deducted.
Is gratuity included in CTC?
Yes. Most employers include gratuity in CTC — typically 4.81% of Basic salary (calculated as 15/26 × monthly Basic). However, gratuity is only paid when you complete 5 or more years of continuous service. If you leave before 5 years, you do not receive this amount, yet it was part of your CTC.
