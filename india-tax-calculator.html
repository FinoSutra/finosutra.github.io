Frequently Asked Questions — Income Tax FY 2026-27

Which tax regime is better — New or Old for FY 2026-27?
It depends on your deductions. The New Regime is usually better if your total deductions (80C + 80D + HRA + home loan interest etc.) are below approximately ₹3.75 lakh. For most salaried individuals with PF, PPF, LIC, and home loans, the Old Regime often wins. Use this calculator's "Compute & Compare" feature to see the exact difference — it runs both regimes simultaneously with your actual numbers.
What is the Section 87A rebate for FY 2026-27?
Under the New Regime for FY 2026-27, taxpayers with total income up to ₹12,00,000 get a full rebate u/s 87A — meaning zero tax payable. Under the Old Regime, the rebate is ₹12,500 for income up to ₹5,00,000. The rebate is not available on special-rate income like LTCG from equity.
Is this calculator valid for AY 2027-28?
Yes. This calculator covers FY 2026-27 (April 2026 to March 2027), which corresponds to Assessment Year (AY) 2027-28. The ITR for FY 2026-27 will be filed in AY 2027-28 (July 2027 deadline for most taxpayers).
What is the advance tax due date for Q1 FY 2026-27?
The first advance tax instalment (15% of estimated tax liability) is due by 15 June 2026. Subsequent instalments: 45% by 15 Sep 2026, 75% by 15 Dec 2026, and 100% by 15 Mar 2027. Salaried individuals whose entire tax is deducted as TDS are generally exempt from advance tax. Non-compliance attracts interest under Sections 234B and 234C.
