Frequently Asked Questions — Advance Tax

What is advance tax?
Advance tax is income tax paid in quarterly instalments during the financial year, rather than as one lump sum after year-end, wherever the estimated tax liability for the year exceeds ₹10,000.
What happens if I don't pay advance tax on time?
Late or short payment of advance tax attracts interest under Section 234B (for shortfall in total advance tax paid) and Section 234C (for deferment of individual instalments), each charged at 1% per month on the shortfall.
